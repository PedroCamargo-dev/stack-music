**Music Stack** — a local-first app for building and tracking your music library, gear, and listening goals in one place.

- Catalog albums, vinyl, and digital releases with metadata and cover art
- Track gear (instruments, pedals, synths) with value and maintenance logs
- Set and log listening goals, reviews, and ratings per release
- Visualize collection stats over time (genres, formats, spending)
- Export/import data as JSON for full ownership, no cloud dependence
